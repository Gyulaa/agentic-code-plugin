# The tells of generic, machine-made design

Each of these is legitimate when the brief asks for it. The fault is when it appears without a decision. After removing one, check that you did not just move to the next most probable default.

| Area | Tell |
|---|---|
| Colour | an indigo or purple gradient on white; cream background with a serif and terracotta accent (now its own default); near-black with one acid-green or vermilion accent; pure grey neutrals instead of neutrals tinted toward the brand hue; grey text on a coloured background |
| Type | Inter, Roboto, Arial or the system font with no reason; Space Grotesk as the "not Inter" choice; one highlighted word in the headline; an all-caps eyebrow label above every heading; monospace as a "technical" costume |
| Layout | a centred hero over three equal cards (icon, title, two lines); cards inside cards; the same radius and shadow on everything; "big number, small label, gradient" stat rows; 01 / 02 / 03 numbering where there is no sequence; hairline-ruled newspaper layout for a product page |
| Surface | gradient text; decorative glassmorphism; a thick coloured left border on cards; hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0`) outside a deliberate neo-brutalist system; dark glows |
| Motion | the same fade-and-slide-up on every section; hover animation on every card; spring or elastic easing on UI; `transition: all` |
| Imagery | generic stock (handshakes, laptops on desks, smiling headsets); abstract 3D blobs; AI-generated people presented as customers or staff |
| Copy | "John Doe" and "Acme"; sales talk instead of description; `A · B · C` metadata lines; `→` at the end of every button; emoji as icons; the inflated vocabulary in `copy.md` |

## Native tells
- **iOS app that feels ported from the web:** a custom navigation bar, swipe-back disabled, hover-dependent controls, web-style toasts and modals, hand-drawn blur instead of system materials, fixed text sizes.
- **Android app wearing an iOS skin:** iOS-style back chevrons and tab bars, no predictive back, ignoring dynamic colour and Material roles.
- **A Material app on iPhone** that skips what iOS users expect: safe areas, Reduce Motion, swipe-back, the system share sheet.

## The self-check
1. Would I give the same answer for any similar brief? If yes, the direction is generic.
2. What on this screen could only belong to this client? If nothing, add it from the subject (their photos, their data, their vocabulary, their place).
3. Where is the one bold move? If everything is bold, nothing is; if nothing is, the page is forgettable.
4. Take one accessory off (the Chanel rule): the extra shadow, the second accent, the decorative icon.
